How to play

  • Tap a bud to give it a petal. Each tap uses one drop.
  • At 4 petals it blooms and sends a petal along each of its arrows.
  • Glowing buds have 3 petals. A petal landing there makes them bloom too, so chains run across the garden.
  • Chains of 4+ give a drop back, 8+ two, 12+ three. Longer chains score far more pollen.
  • When your drops run out, the garden rests. Plan the longest chains you can.
